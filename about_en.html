About

Research, modelling and public expertise

Adrien Fauste-Gay

I hold a PhD from Université Grenoble Alpes, completed at ISTerre and CIRED. I am now a modelling officer in the public affairs team at The Shift Project, working on European energy sovereignty. Trained in physics and biophysics, I now study the material basis of agricultural and energy systems, their vulnerabilities and transformations.

My work sits at the interface of physical sciences, quantitative environmental history, modelling and foresight. I aim to build tools transparent enough to be debated, reused and applied beyond academia.
