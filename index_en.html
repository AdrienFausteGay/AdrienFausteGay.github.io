Biophysical modelling of agricultural and energy systems

I develop and use flow-based models to study how the material structure of systems shapes their performance, vulnerabilities, historical trajectories and possible futures. I hold a PhD from Université Grenoble Alpes, where I studied French agricultural metabolism from 1852 to 2050. I now work on European energy sovereignty.
