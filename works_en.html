Research

Understanding systems through their flows

My work combines biophysical accounting, network science, historical analysis and foresight to understand what system structure makes possible, efficient or vulnerable.

01 / Composition & vulnerability

Linking agricultural composition to performance

Using detailed nitrogen-flow networks, I study how crop mixes and herd structure are associated with agro-environmental efficiency and distinct forms of metabolic vulnerability across 33 French territories from 1852 to 2014.

SSRN preprint
Dendrogram of the inductive typology: observed systems are grouped by similarity in their metabolic signatures.
02 / Quantitative environmental history

Reconstructing 162 years of agricultural change

Unsupervised classification identifies recurring biophysical structures and reconstructs a metabolic history of French regional agriculture. Over- and under-represented transitions are consistent with forms of path dependence.

Historical dataset
03 / Agricultural foresight

Putting contrasted visions in a common physical framework

I compare 17 stakeholder visions for French agriculture in 2050 using E-GRAFS to compare assumptions and trade-offs involving livestock, diets, inputs, trade and competing uses of biomass.

Scenario data

An article presenting this comparison is under review at Agricultural Systems.

One of the metabolic-phase classification trees presented in the thesis appendix.
04 / Chemical-network formalism

Exploring feasible regimes beyond fixed scenarios

With Jérémie Unterberger, we reformulate agro-food nitrogen metabolism as a continuous-time flow network inspired by chemical reaction networks, allowing us to study metabolic phases and transitions between dominant recycling cycles.

arXiv preprint HAL record
05 / Energy · 2026, ongoing

Multisectoral energy dependence in the EU-27

As a modelling officer in the public affairs team at The Shift Project, I work on European energy sovereignty through a multisectoral model of energy dependence across the 27 EU Member States. This extends my work on material dependencies beyond agriculture.
